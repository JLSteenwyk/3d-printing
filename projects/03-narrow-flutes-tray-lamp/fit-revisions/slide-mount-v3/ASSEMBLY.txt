With the cord unplugged and bulb removed, lower the bracket into the open top. Slide its base from the right-hand opening toward the closed stop on the left. Hold the latch outward if needed, then let it return behind the bracket's front edge. Verify that the bracket seats flat and cannot slide back out with the latch engaged.

Install the cord anchor and two snap pins as in the accepted test assembly. Route the cord with slack behind the socket and secure it through the anchor slots without crushing the jacket. Confirm that the pin tips clear the table and cord pulls do not move the socket. Screw in the bulb only after the bracket is seated.
