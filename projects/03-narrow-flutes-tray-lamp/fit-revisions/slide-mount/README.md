# Bolt-free slide mount trial

Print one [receiver](../../models/slide_receiver_test_PETG.3mf) and two [snap pins](../../models/anchor_snap_pin_PETG_print_two.3mf) in PETG at 100% scale. These are geometry-only 3MF files; printer settings and supports are not included. Inspect rail lips in Bambu Studio.

Reuse the existing socket bracket and cord anchor. Rails capture the bracket base; an integral lateral spring tab prevents withdrawal. Two separately printed snap pins secure the cord anchor through its existing holes. The receiver is one connected print; it is not a preassembled moving mechanism. Supports may be needed at rail lips.

Follow [print and test instructions](PRINT_AND_TEST.txt) and [diagram](slide-mount-diagram.png). Geometry checks passed. Physical sliding clearance, latch retention, pin fit and durability are pending. The receiver has not been integrated into the full lamp yet. Do not print the old bolted body for this option until the test is accepted and the body is updated. Test unplugged.
