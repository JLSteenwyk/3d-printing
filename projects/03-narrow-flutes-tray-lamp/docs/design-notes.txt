NARROW FLUTES V2 — SINGLE OLIVE PRINT, SEPARATE TAN TRIM

V2 changes
The olive wall, lower frame, socket-adapter pad, supporting ledge and recessed
tray receiver are one connected PETG model. There are no olive assembly joints,
separate feet, or screws between the diffuser and receiver. The tan base and
top sleeves print separately and slip on afterward, with removable adhesive
tabs if needed. The socket, its adapter, cord and grip pads install afterward.

Appearance
V1's ribs were only 1.6 mm deep and looked like shallow corrugations. V2 uses
rounded 7 mm-wide ribs with 3.4 mm relief and approximately 8.4 mm spacing.
Their upper ends are rounded; lower 45-degree chamfers are hidden by tan trim.
The front and right faces are uninterrupted. Intake and exhaust windows are
on the rear and left end. The continuous ventilation gap of V1 is removed.
This model follows the selected concept's round rib texture; it cannot promise
the AI image's exact glow or unmeasured proportions. The preview renders actual
CAD geometry with a schematic tray. It does not simulate PETG transparency.

Dimensions
Olive body: 247.23 x 207.86 x 152 mm. With tan trim: 251.23 x 211.86 mm.
Tray pocket: 241.23 x 201.86 mm, 8 mm deep, 0.6 mm rigid clearance per side.
Tray sits at 144 mm; total height with the supplied 25.4 mm tray is 169.4 mm.
Tray corner radius is still an assumed 15 mm, not a measured value.
Grip pads: four 24 x 4.5 mm recesses, 0.3 mm deep. Start with compressible
adhesive pads near 1 mm thick and adjust according to actual fit.
The tray is retained against lateral motion, but is deliberately lift-out.
Tan sleeves have 0.3 mm radial slip clearance around the olive receiver/body.
They are cosmetic and do not replace the PETG load-bearing structure.

Printing intent
Print olive_body_one_piece_PETG.stl upright, flat base on the bed, using ordinary
mode, not vase mode. It is designed to avoid long internal bridges: the ledge
grows inward over a 16 mm slope; vent and cord-opening roofs close at 45 degrees.
The base rails begin on the bed. The bulb can be installed and replaced through
the large tray opening. No permanently trapped printed supports are intended.
The remaining small transitions still need inspection in Bambu Studio. No
P2S toolpath, support setting or physical print has been validated. One connected
solid does not by itself prove successful support-free printing.
Tan trim fits the nominal 256 mm build volume but is 251.23 mm wide, leaving
only about 2.38 mm per edge. Inspect brim/plate placement carefully.
Use your PETG filament profile and test adhesion before the large body print.

First print
Print tray_corner_coupon.stl to check the assumed corner, then print the full
tray_fit_test_rim.stl to check the opposing sides. The fit samples reproduce the
V2 receiver geometry. Adjust parameters.json and regenerate before printing
the full lamp if the bare rim binds. Add pads after confirming the rigid fit.

Socket and thermal status
One horizontal A19 bulb envelope (123 x 60 mm), with a provisional 45 x 45 mm
socket envelope beside it, clears the model. This is a space reservation, not
the actual hardware. The socket mount/adapter and strain relief remain to be
designed around a selected complete prewired E26 socket-and-cord assembly.
The integral mounting pad has four 3.4 mm holes on a 40 x 24 mm pattern.
No printed electrical contacts are supplied. Do not energize this incomplete
prototype. Final hardware retention, access and cord routing need review.
There are 14 upper vent windows and 14 lower windows; tan trim does not seal
them. Adequate temperatures with the leather tray installed are not established.
The H6008 label prohibits use in totally enclosed luminaires. Final heat and
load/creep testing with the intended contents are still required.

Verification
All five exported designs are one connected, watertight, consistently wound
solid. They fit the nominal 256 mm build envelope. Tan trim has no material
overlap with the olive body; the reserved hardware envelope has no intersection.
overhang-screen.json records geometric layer-growth screening at 0.2 mm pitch;
this is not slicing or proof that supports are unnecessary. validation.json
records model checks and explicitly marks unfinished physical/slicer tests.

Editing
generate.py is the parametric CAD source; parameters.json holds tray dimensions,
clearance and main dimensions. Dependencies: numpy, manifold3d, trimesh, Pillow.
Run python generate.py. Some rib geometry, vent positions, socket pad and ledge
features are fixed prototype values in the source; review those after changing
parameters. The published project contains the selected V2 revision.
